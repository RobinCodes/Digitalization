/ accounts

Viewer accounts

People with an account can sign in on the site and read notes you've marked members only. Anonymous visitors never see those notes. These are viewer accounts — they cannot reach DevTools.

Passwords are stored only as a random salt + scrypt hash, never in plain text. Re-adding an existing username updates its password.
