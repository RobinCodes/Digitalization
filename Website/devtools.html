/ accounts

Viewer accounts

People with an account can sign in on the site and read notes you've marked members only. Anonymous visitors never see those notes. These are viewer accounts — they cannot reach DevTools.

Passwords are stored only as a random salt + scrypt hash, never in plain text. Re-adding an existing username updates its password.

Back up

The day log, the messages, the accounts, the access grants and every scan you have uploaded live only on this server — the repository does not hold them. Download a copy somewhere safe, and do it before any move or upgrade.

State & uploads is the one you want regularly: the JSON stores plus Uploads/. Everything adds Data/, DataHU/ and the article pages, which are normally in git already. The archive includes a README-restore.txt explaining where each file goes back.
The zip carries salted password hashes. Keep it where you would keep a password-manager export.
/ timetable

The timetable

The recurring weekly schedule. Everything below feeds the public Days page and pre-fills the lesson list whenever you log a day, so it is worth getting right once.

01 Setup

Which days you have lessons on, whether the week alternates (A/B), and the school-year window the public calendar defaults to.

Members only — hide the timetable and its calendar from signed-out visitors

02 Periods

The rows of the grid. A label plus (optionally) start and end times — the times show on the public timetable and on each logged lesson.

03 Subjects

Every subject you can place in the grid. The colour is used everywhere the subject appears. Linking a subject to a folder of digital notes makes that folder one click away when you log a lesson.

04 The week

Click an empty cell to place a lesson, or a lesson to edit or remove it.

05 Holidays & special dates

Date ranges with no lessons (or with something else going on). They appear on the public calendar and warn you before you log a day that falls inside one.
